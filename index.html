KlinCAD

A lightweight electronic design automation tool for learning electronics, designing circuits and working with PCBs without the complexity of professional EDA suites.

Designed to make electronics approachable

KlinCAD focuses on reducing unnecessary complexity so students, teachers and beginners can concentrate on understanding electronics and circuit design.

PCB design

Design and route PCBs in an interactive environment.

Component workflow

Select, place and work with electronic components intuitively.

Multi-layer boards

Work with multiple PCB layers for more advanced designs.

Design-rule checking

Use built-in tools to identify potential design problems.

Spanish & English

An interface designed to be accessible to users in both languages.
